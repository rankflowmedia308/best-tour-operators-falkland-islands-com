About This Guide

An independent editorial resource comparing tour operators for the Falkland Islands.

What This Site Is

best-tour-operators-falkland-islands.com is an independent editorial resource that researches, compares, and ranks tour operators running trips to the Falkland Islands. We cover both expedition cruise operators and Stanley-based local guides.

We are not affiliated with, owned by, or compensated by any operator listed on this site. No operator has paid for placement, a review, or any form of editorial coverage. Rankings reflect our independent scoring methodology.

Why We Built This

The Falkland Islands are remote, logistically complex, and home to wildlife encounters that rank among the best in the world. Yet information about the operators who run trips there is scattered across cruise review sites, travel forums, and tourism board listings — without any independent synthesis.

We built this site to be the resource we wished existed: a clear comparison of every credible operator, with consistent criteria, transparent scoring, and enough depth to support a real booking decision.

Our Research Method

For each operator, we:

  • Verified active listings against the Falkland Islands Tourist Board operator registry (falklandislands.com)
  • Cross-referenced guest review scores on TourRadar and Cruise Critic
  • Checked IAATO membership status for expedition cruise operators
  • Reviewed operator websites, itinerary documents, and pricing pages
  • Consulted traveller reports from Falklands-focused forums and expedition review sites

We do not conduct sponsored press trips or accept complimentary berths. All research is desk-based and publicly verifiable.

Editorial Independence

No operator on this site has paid for their listing, their ranking position, or any content written about them. If we accept advertising in future, it will be clearly labelled and separated from editorial content.

Our editorial policy is detailed in full on the Editorial Policy page. Our scoring methodology — including criteria weights — is published in full on the Methodology page.
